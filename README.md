# house-rules


## House Rules


**Investigating Knowledge**
- If your Exploration Action is **Investigate** on your first turn in combat you can attempt to **Recall Knowledge** as a free action

**Ronald's Rules of Aid** 
- Aid DC = DC of the check you aid
  - **Crit Success** `+4`
  - **Success** `+2`
  - **Failure** `+1`
  - **Critical Failure** `-1`
- _Bonuses to checks to aid (ie cooperative nature) are generally slashed in half_

**Follow the Trained**
- `Follow the Expert` now also works for trained skills granting a `+1` boost

**Heroic Crafting**
- Using the `Heroic Crafting` variant system to replace core crafting

**Find an Ally**
 - Spend 1 action to find an adjacent ally if you're blinded to ignore the flat check (this is lost if either of you move)

# Character Building Rules
## Character Building Related House Rules
**Background Lore Scaling**
- Lore from your Background now grants you the [Additional Lore Feat](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=5114&Redirected=1) for that Lore

**[Gradual Ability Boosts](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1300)**
 - Grants ability boosts throughout level up

**Basic Pack**
- Gain the following extra feats
 - at **1st Level**
   - 1 Class Feat
   - 1 General Feat
 - at **2nd Level**
   - 1 Class Feat

## Feature Changes
**Make Higher Level Saves More Interesting**
- When you roll a success on the saving throw, you get a critical success instead. ⇒ When you roll a **critical failure** on the saving throw, you get a **failure** instead.
- When you roll a critical failure on the saving throw, you get a failure instead. ⇒ When you roll a **success** on the saving throw, you get a **critical success** instead.

## Feat Changes
**[Garden of Healing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2166)**
- In Exploration Mode the total healing received is `1d8 per spell rank`
  - _This puts it in line with other spells_
- Add the following to the description. _You can sustain the spell if cast in combat 1 more time post combat_

**[Rippling Spin](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=7499&NoRedirect=1)**
- **Traits:** Flourish, Monk, Occult, Water ⇒ **~~Flourish,~~ Monk, Occult, Water**

**[Timber Sentinel](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=4284)**
- **Traits:** Impulse, Kineticist, Plant, Primal, Wood ⇒ **Impulse, Kineticist, __Overflow__, Plant, Primal, Wood**
